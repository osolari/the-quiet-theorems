Hoeffding's covariance identity (Probability Notes No. 00): [link]

Hoeffding (1940) showed that
Cov(X,Y) = ∬ [F_XY(x,y) − F_X(x)F_Y(y)] dx dy

X and Y are independent exactly when the bracket, joint CDF minus product of marginals, is zero everywhere.

2/2
So independence requires the bracket to vanish everywhere, while zero covariance requires only that its positive and negative parts cancel. If it has a fixed sign, zero covariance does imply independence (Lehmann, 1966).
