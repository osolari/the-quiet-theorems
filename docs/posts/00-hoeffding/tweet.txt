Hoeffding's covariance identity (Probability Notes No. 00): https://omidsolari.substack.com/p/probability-notes-no-00-hoeffdings

Hoeffding (1940): Cov(X,Y) = ∬ [F_XY(x,y) − F_X(x)F_Y(y)] dx dy.

X and Y are independent iff the bracket is zero everywhere; zero covariance only needs it to integrate to zero.
