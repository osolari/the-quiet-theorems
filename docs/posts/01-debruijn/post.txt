De Bruijn's identity (The Quiet Theorems No. 01): [link]

Add Gaussian noise of variance t to X. How fast does its entropy grow?

De Bruijn (Stam, 1959) showed that
d/dt h(X + √t Z) = ½ J(X + √t Z)

h is differential entropy and J is Fisher information.

2/2
So Fisher information is the speed at which Gaussian smoothing creates entropy, even for discrete X. Since 1/(σ²+t) ≤ J ≤ 1/t, Gaussian inputs gain entropy most slowly. The identity is also dI/dsnr = ½ mmse (Guo et al., 2005).
