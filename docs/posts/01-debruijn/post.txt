De Bruijn's identity (The Quiet Theorems No. 01): https://omidsolari.substack.com/p/the-quiet-theorems-no-01-de-bruijns

Add Gaussian noise of variance t to X. How fast does its entropy grow?

De Bruijn (published by Stam, 1959):
d/dt h(X + √t Z) = ½ J(X + √t Z)

h is differential entropy and J is Fisher information.

2/2
So entropy grows at half the Fisher information of the noisy variable, even for discrete X. If Var X = σ², then 1/(σ²+t) ≤ J ≤ 1/t, so Gaussian inputs gain entropy most slowly. Equivalently, dI/dsnr = ½ mmse (Guo, Shamai and Verdú, 2005).
