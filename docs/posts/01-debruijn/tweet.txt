De Bruijn's identity (The Quiet Theorems No. 01): https://omidsolari.substack.com/p/the-quiet-theorems-no-01-de-bruijns

Add Gaussian noise of variance t to X. Entropy grows at half the Fisher information (de Bruijn; Stam, 1959):

d/dt h(X + √t Z) = ½ J(X + √t Z)

Fisher information sets the rate of entropy growth.
